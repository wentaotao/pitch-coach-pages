Pitch Coach

Support

Help for clearer, more reliable pitch practice.

Requirements: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Pitch Coach is designed for portrait orientation.

Microphone permission

Pitch Coach needs microphone access to detect pitch. If access was denied, open iOS Settings → Apps → Pitch Coach → Microphone, enable access, then return to the app.

Improve detection

  • Place the iPhone on a stable surface about one arm away.
  • Practice in a quiet room and move away from fans, televisions, or speakers.
  • Sing one clear note at a time during Match, Hold, and Scale exercises.
  • Use the built-in iPhone microphone when possible. Bluetooth microphones can add latency.

Daily reminders

Enable or disable reminders in Pitch Coach Settings. If notification permission is denied, enable it later in iOS Settings.

Delete practice data

Open Settings → Clear all data and confirm. This removes locally stored sessions, streaks, badges, note counts, and vocal-range records. Delete the app to remove all remaining preferences.

Privacy

Audio is processed on-device and never uploaded. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Contact support

Open a GitHub issue and include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not post voice recordings or sensitive information.
