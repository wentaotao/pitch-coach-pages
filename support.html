Before contacting support

  • Confirm microphone access is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Practice in a quiet room with the iPhone about one arm away.
  • Use the built-in iPhone microphone when possible; Bluetooth microphones may add latency.
  • Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and PitchCoachPro version when reporting an issue.
