Pitch Coach

Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Pitch Coach works entirely on your iPhone. We do not create accounts, show ads, track you, or upload your audio or practice data.

Who operates Pitch Coach

Pitch Coach is provided by its independent developer. For privacy questions, open a support request on GitHub.

Microphone access

Pitch Coach uses the microphone only while you run a pitch exercise or room-noise check. Audio samples are processed in memory on your device to estimate pitch and signal quality. Raw audio is not recorded, saved to a file, or transmitted off your device.

Information stored on your device

The app may store derived practice information locally, including session duration, scores, pitch accuracy and stability, sampled pitch curves, vocal range, streaks, badges, reminder preferences, note-naming preferences, and microphone-check results.

Practice sessions older than 180 days may be removed automatically. You can delete practice history using Settings → Clear all data. Other preferences can be removed by deleting the app.

Notifications

If you enable a daily reminder, Pitch Coach schedules a local notification on your device. The reminder is not sent by our servers and can be disabled in the app or iOS Settings.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, share, or use personal data for advertising or tracking. Pitch Coach has no account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or runtime network service.

Third-party software

Pitch Coach uses SwiftF0 and ONNX Runtime for on-device pitch inference. These components process data locally and do not transmit your audio to their developers.

Children's privacy

Because no personal data is collected, Pitch Coach does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Open a GitHub issue for privacy questions or support.
