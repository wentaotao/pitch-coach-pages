PitchCoachPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

PitchCoachPro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party sign-in services.

Microphone access

PitchCoachPro uses the microphone only while you run a pitch exercise or room-noise check. Audio is processed in memory on your device to estimate pitch and signal quality. Raw audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Data stored on your device

The app stores derived practice statistics and preferences locally on your device. This may include session scores, pitch accuracy and stability, sampled pitch curves, vocal range, streaks, badges, reminder settings, and microphone-check results. This data is not transmitted by the app.

You can remove practice history in Settings by selecting Clear all data. Deleting the app removes all remaining local data.

Notifications

If enabled, daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are not sent by a server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
